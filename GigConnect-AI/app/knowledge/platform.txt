GigConnect Platform Guide

GigConnect is a freelancing marketplace.

Client Workflow

1. Register/Login
2. Complete Client Profile
3. Post a Job
4. Receive Bids
5. Review Bids
6. Accept One Bid
7. Project is Created
8. Review Submitted Work
9. Make Payment using Razorpay
10. Give Rating & Review

Freelancer Workflow

1. Register/Login
2. Complete Freelancer Profile
3. Browse Jobs
4. Submit Bid
5. Wait for Client Acceptance
6. Work on Project
7. Submit Work
8. Receive Payment
9. View Payment History
10. Receive Rating

Features

JWT Authentication
WebSocket Chat
Razorpay Payments
Real-time Messaging
Ratings & Reviews
